How I sell

From the first hello to the customer who refers a friend.

The same five steps whether it's a software subscription, a solar install or a kitchen remodel.

  1. 01

    Prospect

    Find and qualify the right people, so time goes to buyers who are a real fit.

    Crystal: from zero to 1,750+ customers

  2. 02

    Discover

    Ask before pitching: what they need, what it's worth to them, and who makes the call.

    Consulting: needs-first consultations

  3. 03

    Present

    Recommend the right option and explain the price in plain terms, with no surprises later.

    Gamefam: pitched NFL and Intuit executives

  4. 04

    Close

    Answer objections honestly, negotiate fairly, and ask for the business.

    Crystal: personally closed high-value contracts

  5. 05

    Follow up

    Onboard, check in and fix problems fast. Customers who stay are the ones who refer.

    Crystal: fast issue resolution kept customers
